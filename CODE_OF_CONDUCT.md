# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project exists to make cybersecurity education more accessible. That
mission requires a welcoming environment for learners at every level,
including complete beginners.

Be respectful. Assume good faith, especially from beginners asking
questions that might seem basic. Disagree with ideas, not people. No
harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks — including against other
educators, courses, or projects (see `README.md`: this project's message is
"education should be accessible," not an attack on anyone charging for
their own work).

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
official project communication channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open an issue marked
confidential/private if the platform supports it, or contact the
maintainers directly through the contact method listed in the repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors
for repeated or severe violations.
