# Challenge — Module 10

Take a real system you use daily (your phone, your home Wi-Fi, your email account) and produce a short written "attack surface inventory": list every distinct thing an attacker could potentially interact with (open ports if you know them, login pages, installed apps with network access, physical access points, etc.). For each item, note one concrete way you could reduce that specific piece of attack surface without losing functionality you actually need — this is the same reasoning Module 17 (Defensive Security) formalizes later.
