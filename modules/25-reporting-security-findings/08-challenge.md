# Challenge — Module 25

Produce a complete, professional mini-report for your Module 26 lab environment, covering:
- Executive Summary
- Scope (what you tested, what you didn't)
- At least two findings from your Module 11–20 labs (vsftpd CVE, anonymous FTP, SMB shares — your actual enumeration results)
- Each finding in the full template format from `01-concepts.md`
- A conclusion section: overall risk posture and top three recommended actions

This is the same structure a professional penetration test report uses. The difference between this challenge and the professional version is primarily scope and depth — the format and discipline are identical.
