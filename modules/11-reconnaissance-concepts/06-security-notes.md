# Security Notes — Module 11

- Port scanning a system you don't own, even a "quick check," is unauthorized access preparation in many jurisdictions — treat scope as absolute, not a suggestion.
- Aggressive scan settings can degrade or crash fragile or older services — this is a real operational risk even in *authorized* engagements, which is why professional recon usually starts conservative and escalates deliberately.
- From a defensive standpoint, unexpected inbound scan traffic in your own logs is a meaningful detection signal — Module 17 covers what to look for.
