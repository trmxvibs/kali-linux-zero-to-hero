# Lab 29 — Your Personal Development Plan

**Environment:** A text editor — this is a planning exercise.

## Steps

1. Write down the career path from `01-concepts.md` that most aligns with what you want to do.
2. List three specific skills from this course you feel strongest in, and three you feel weakest in.
3. Pick one free resource from `01-concepts.md`'s table and commit to spending one hour per week on it for the next month.
4. Set up a HackTheBox or TryHackMe account if you haven't already; complete one machine or room before finishing this module.
5. Look at this course's CONTRIBUTING.md and identify one module you could contribute content to — whether a typo fix, an improved exercise, or a full lesson.

## Expected Result

A written personal development plan — dated, specific, and realistic. Revisit it in 30 days.
