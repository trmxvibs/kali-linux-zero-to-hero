# Lab 23 — Your First CTF Challenge

**Environment:** Browser + any Linux terminal. No VM needed.

## Steps

1. Go to [picoCTF](https://picoctf.org/) and create a free account (no payment required).
2. Open the Practice arena (always-on challenges, not a timed competition).
3. Pick ONE challenge from a category covered in this course — Web, Forensics, or Cryptography are the most directly mapped. Start with a beginner-level challenge.
4. Before touching any tool, write down:
   - What type of challenge is this? (the category)
   - What is given? (a file, a URL, a snippet of text?)
   - What is the flag format? (usually shown in the challenge description)
5. Apply the four-step methodology from `01-concepts.md`.
6. Solve the challenge and find the flag.
7. Write a one-paragraph "mini write-up" of what you did, what you tried that failed, and what worked — this is the discipline that becomes Module 25's reporting habit.

## Expected Result

A found flag and a written record of your approach. The specific challenge doesn't matter; the methodology and write-up habit do.
