# Exercises — Module 19

1. Explain the difference between WEP, WPA2-PSK, and WPA3 in terms of the specific weakness each addresses vs. the one before it.
2. Why does a strong password make the WPA2 handshake-capture attack infeasible, if the handshake was captured successfully?
3. On your own home/lab Wi-Fi setup, check which protocol you're currently using and, if it's WPA2, confirm your password length — then explain whether you're satisfied with your current exposure to the offline dictionary attack.
4. Explain, in your own words, why "scanning for networks with airodump-ng" is legally and ethically different from "capturing a handshake from a network you don't own."
